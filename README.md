Welcome to the **LeetCode Feedback** repository!

This is the official feedback channel for reporting **content-related issues** on [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com) or [LeetCode China](https://leetcode.cn).

By submitting issues here, you help us improve the quality and clarity of our platform for millions of users.

---

## 📝 What Types of Issues Can Be Reported?

### 🔹 Problem Content Issues
- Incorrect or unclear **problem descriptions**
- Errors in **examples**
- Missing or invalid **constraints**
- Issues with **hints**
- Misclassified **related topics**
- **Incorrect test cases**
- **Missing test cases**

### 🔸 Editorial Content Issues
- Missing **solution approaches**
- Errors in **editorial videos**
- **Non-working editorial code**
- Broken or confusing **figures/animations**

---

## 🚫 What Not to Report Here

Please do **not** report the following in this repository:

- Billing or account issues  
- Website or feature bugs  
- Contest scoring or dispute resolution  

➡️ For these issues, please use our [Help Center](https://support.leetcode.com/hc/en-us).

---

## 🚀 How to Submit Feedback

1. Go to the [Issues tab](https://github.com/LeetCode-Feedback/LeetCode-Feedback/issues).
2. Click **"New Issue"**.
3. Select an appropriate template:
   - `Bug Report`
   - `Missing Test Case`
   - `Editorial Issue`
4. Fill in the required details.
5. Submit your issue!

We greatly appreciate your contributions to improving LeetCode! 💙
